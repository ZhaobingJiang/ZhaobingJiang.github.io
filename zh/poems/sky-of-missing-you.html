THE SKY OF MISSING YOU
Days those you don't be here
Are always miserable
Nights those you walks in my dreams
Are always happy
I couldn't help to missing you
Even I don't know
How to express my feeling towards you
Always I want to
Hear your voice on the line
Always I want to
Touch your hands in my mind
And always I want to
Glance your smiles on the road
I don't mind I love you
Even not be loved in return
I don't mind you ever made me crazy
Just wait for love to grow in my heart
I don't mind your ignoreness what being in love is
As long as I love you day by day
'Cause I feel that you are more myself than I am
And I always love you forever
——OWEN, 29 November 2004
