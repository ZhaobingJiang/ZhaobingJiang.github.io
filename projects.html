Projects

Since 2017, Nanjing Opatiya Information Technology Co., Ltd. has focused on numerical simulation and intelligent prediction, undertaking more than 120 projects commissioned by research institutes, universities, and enterprises across four major areas: AI+CAE, deeply integrating deep learning with CFD-based numerical simulation to make simulation smarter and more automated; waterway hydrology, including water level forecasting for the Yangtze River and deep learning-based terrain prediction for the 12.5-meter deep waterway that supports intelligent shipping along the Yangtze trunk line; aviation meteorology, covering airport wind field forecasting, aircraft icing diagnostics, and 3D glide-path wind field inversion; and CFD software and fluid simulation, including OpenFOAM-based indoor flow field simulation, a fluid simulation cloud platform, and fluid-structure interaction studies of pontoon bridges and cross-medium UAVs.
